# Consent form draft for `simpanugm-eval` trial

Internal draft. Move this wording into the team's approved consent form before asking
anyone to participate. Do not commit completed forms or participant contact details to
this repository.

## Purpose

We are collecting photographs and descriptions of everyday objects to evaluate the
SimpanUGM lost-and-found workflow. The material will be used for offline evaluation and
the course report only.

## What participation involves

- lending one or more ordinary objects for the trial;
- allowing a consenting team member to photograph the object in a campus-like setting;
- writing a short owner description from memory and reviewing the recorded labels;
- allowing the resulting de-identified item record and images to be stored in the private
  team Drive folder.

## Safety and exclusions

- No real identity cards, bank cards, account numbers, medical records, or other
  sensitive documents may be submitted.
- Mock cards must follow [`mock-identity-card.md`](mock-identity-card.md).
- No faces, hands, reflections, screens with personal data, addresses, or other people
  may appear in photographs.
- EXIF metadata is removed before upload.

## Use, access, and retention

The trial material is restricted to the three project members in the private team Drive
folder. It is used for matching and report-assistance evaluation and the course report
until 31 December 2027. A participant may request deletion of their contribution before
that date through the team contact recorded in the approved form. The dataset is not
published, sold, or used for a purpose outside the project without new consent.

## Confirmation fields

The approved form should record only what is needed to manage consent:

- participant code;
- consent date;
- confirmation that the participant read the purpose, risks, access, retention, and
  deletion terms;
- confirmation that the contributed object and images contain no prohibited data;
- typed name or signature, stored outside this repository.

Participation is voluntary. A participant may decline or withdraw without academic or
project penalty.
