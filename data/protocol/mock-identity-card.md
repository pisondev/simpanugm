# Mock identity-card specification

Identity-category examples in the trial use props only. The props are designed to test
handling of sensitive-looking objects without exposing real personal data.

## Required properties

- Use a fictitious name and fictitious institution details.
- Print `CONTOH` prominently across the front and back.
- Use a card number beginning with `0000` and never reuse a real number.
- Use a fictional portrait or no portrait; never use a participant's face.
- Do not use the UGM logo, a real bank logo, or a real government emblem.
- Record the internal mock-card code separately from the photographed card.
- Destroy the paper prop after the trial or keep it in the team's controlled storage.

## Photographing the prop

Photograph it like an ordinary found item, but keep the mock status visible. Check the
image for faces, reflections, real names, real numbers, screens, and addresses before
uploading. The intake check must remove EXIF metadata.

The mock card is test material, not proof of identity and never a substitute for the
real-world verification process described in the product requirements.
